These notes study human-agent coordination and resource allocation through
digital tokenomics as part of a verified decentralized superintelligence
(DSI) system. We develop its models, specifications, executable operations, and
proofs natively in [Lean 4](https://lean-lang.org/).

The current library implements participant registration and snapshot merging,
with proofs of registry uniqueness and preservation of saved registrations in
a system execution model. A TCP prototype runs independent nodes on one machine.
It has no ledger or payment implementation.

[Identifier collision probability](../VDSI/IdGeneration/Note.mdx) develops a
finite sampling model and proves a risk bound for independent uniform IDs.
The runtime still accepts manually supplied IDs; a random generator is not
implemented. The earlier models and notes are available in the
[archived version](https://github.com/CloK-Lab/vdsi/tree/43f26201474a144b68924d2b485a0e3fd857c483).
